# WorkNearMe
A location-based marketplace that connects customers with nearby skilled service providers.

## Overview

WorkNearMe is a location-based service marketplace that connects service providers with customers seeking nearby services.

The platform recommends relevant jobs to service providers based on their skills, location, availability, and reputation, while helping customers find trusted professionals quickly.
