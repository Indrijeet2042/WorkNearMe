# WorkNearMe

## Problem Statement

Millions of skilled workers such as carpenters, electricians, plumbers, painters, mechanics, and daily wage workers spend a significant amount of time searching for work opportunities. 
At the same time, customers often struggle to find reliable and skilled service providers nearby when they need urgent or short-term services.

The current process is largely informal and dependent on local contacts, labor markets, contractors, or word-of-mouth recommendations. 
This creates inefficiencies, limits earning opportunities for workers, increases waiting time, and makes it difficult for customers to find trustworthy professionals.

WorkNearMe aims to bridge this gap by providing a location-based digital marketplace that connects service providers and service consumers through intelligent matching based on skills, 
proximity, availability, experience, and reputation.

---

## Objectives

1. Connect service providers with nearby customers seeking services.
2. Reduce idle waiting time for skilled workers and daily wage earners.
3. Help customers quickly find trustworthy and qualified professionals.
4. Recommend relevant jobs to service providers based on their skills and location.
5. Create a transparent platform with ratings and reviews.
6. Enable direct communication between service providers and customers.
7. Increase employment opportunities for skilled workers.
8. Build a trusted ecosystem through verification and reputation systems.
9. Support both short-term and long-term service engagements.
10. Provide a scalable platform that can support multiple service categories.

## Users

### Service Providers

Service providers are individuals or professionals who offer services and are looking for work opportunities.

Examples include:

* Carpenter
* Electrician
* Plumber
* Painter
* Mechanic
* AC Technician
* Welder
* Mason
* Daily Wage Worker
* House Cleaner
* Tutor
* Gardener
* Driver
* Freelance Service Professionals

### Service Consumers

Service consumers are individuals or organizations seeking services.

Examples include:

* Home Owners
* Tenants
* Shop Owners
* Small Businesses
* Contractors
* Housing Societies
* Offices
* Property Managers

## Features

### Provider Features

* User registration and secure login
* Profile creation and management
* Identity verification
* Add multiple skills and expertise levels
* Specify years of experience
* Set preferred work radius
* Manage availability status
* Receive personalized job recommendations
* View job details and apply for jobs
* Real-time chat with customers
* Accept or reject job requests
* Track work history
* Build reputation through ratings and reviews
* Manage completed and ongoing jobs
* Receive notifications for new opportunities

### Consumer Features

* User registration and secure login
* Create and manage service requests
* Specify job description, budget, and duration
* Upload images related to the job
* View recommended service providers
* Compare provider ratings and experience
* Communicate through real-time chat
* Negotiate service terms
* Select preferred provider
* Track job progress
* Confirm job completion
* Provide ratings and reviews
* View service history
* Receive notifications and updates
